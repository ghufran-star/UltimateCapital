# Next batch: restructure, rules, automation, cost, compliance controls (agreed 24 Oct 2026, waiting for "go")

## Stage 1 — Fast, fits any screen, test mode
1. Instant loading outlines; pages load only what's on screen; lighter deal and list pages; background work no longer slows screens.
2. No page ever scrolls sideways on a phone (only tables, inside their own box). PDFs open fit-to-width. Every screen checked at phone size.
3. Test mode: small (~25 deals, ~30 buyers, every stage once), pre-built, opens instantly, always visible. Pretend calls/texts/email/AI, zero AI or paid-service cost. Replaces the big demo.

## Stage 2 — Obvious to use
4. Side menu: Today · Sellers · Deals · Buyers · Calls · Closing · Settings (Advanced tucked away).
5. Today screen: approvals + recommendations + tasks in one list with big buttons (Approve, Call, Send offer, Fee received).
6. One deal page: progress bar + one "next step" button; plain words ("Most we'll pay" not "max buy").
7. Call buttons wherever a call is needed + a dialer screen.
8. Guided first-time setup.

## Stage 3 — Business rules and data
9. First call: information + the seller's price only; never an offer. Offer comes later.
10. Seller lists: BatchData direct pull + PropStream/BatchLeads file import (auto-recognised columns). State → county → list type, count and cost shown before buying; dedupe, DNC scrub, into a campaign automatically.
11. Cash-buyer lists the same way.
12. Comparables check against list ARVs, only once a seller gives a price.
13. Agents: 7 → 3 (seller caller with stages, buyer caller, receptionist).

## Stage 4 — Automation
14. Follow-up tracks: no answer, not now, price too high, listed with agent, callback, deadline-driven, quiet buyers.
15. "Fee received" button (amount + date) updates everything. Bank feed and CSV upload removed.
16. Deal email setup guide.
17. Cost per deal by list and county.

## Stage 5 — Spend less on AI
18. Prompt caching. 19. Skip AI on empty messages. 20. Batch non-urgent AI work. 21. Call quality checks on a sample. 22. Monthly AI budget with a meter.

## Stage 6 — Compliance controls (owner decides)
23. Switches: AI says it's an AI (on/off), recording notice (on/off), per state if wanted. Logged.
24. Calling hours by where the owner actually lives (mailing address, then phone area code); stricter rule wins. No AI cost.
25. Email templates: builder with designs, merge fields, preview, test send; footer with address + one-click unsubscribe added automatically.
26. Cold outreach mode for uploaded/pulled lists: AI calls + cold texts + emails together. Owner-only switch, off by default, typed confirmation, logged. DNC, litigator, opt-outs, calling hours and daily caps still apply.
27. Hosted consent form ("Get a cash offer"): leads arrive with consent proof saved.
